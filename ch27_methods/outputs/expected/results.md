# Methods

The crystal structure of AmpC beta-lactamase from *Escherichia coli* (PDB 1L2S, 1.94 A, R-free 0.207) was obtained from the RCSB Protein Data Bank. Chain B was used; chain A carries a break at LYS 290, ILE 291, ALA 292 and was discarded. All waters, ions and crystallisation additives were removed, and 10 disordered side chains (REMARK 470) were truncated to the atoms present in the deposited coordinates. The structure contains no metal ion: AmpC is a class C serine hydrolase.

The entry contains 3 copies of the ligand; the copy used (B/2115) was selected by minimum distance to the Ser64 OG atom (2.70 A) rather than by order of appearance in the file, and the remaining copies -- including one 22.72 A from any active site -- were discarded.

Ligands were built from SMILES with explicit formal charges at pH 7.4 (STC -1, 18U -2, 1MU -2), embedded with RDKit ETKDGv3 and optimised with MMFF94s. PDBQT was written but never read back, because the format does not preserve formal charge or atom order.

Docking used AutoDock Vina v1.2.7 with a search box centred at 79.802, 5.352, 29.948 A and measuring 23.74 × 20.60 × 23.18 A, derived from the centroid of the crystallographic ligand with 8 A padding. The random seed was fixed at 42 and exhaustiveness / num_modes / energy_range were 32 / 9 / 3. The seed is reported because Vina's default of 0 selects a random seed, and runs at the default are not reproducible.

Redocking accuracy was assessed as symmetry-corrected heavy-atom RMSD to the crystallographic pose, computed with spyrmsd **without superposition** (minimize=False): 1L2S 1.11 A, 4JXS 3.00 A, 4JXV 10.53 A. Superposition is omitted deliberately; enabling it measures shape agreement rather than placement, and returns 0.000 for a pose displaced from the site.
